Project #6 DATA PORTRAIT

Discription:

I wanted to track my health habits, so I collected data on my water consumption over a week's time. You can click on the text for these seven days to see how many glasses of water I drank each day ( my water glass is 250 ml ).

Reflection:

I used the array and for loop functions to draw the text and the glasses, and if else() to control the appearance of the water by mouse click. Because of the need for this data, I reminded myself to drink water a lot more often this week, so the water consumption is more than usual. But these stats still reflect some of my life, for example, I stayed home on Tuesday and Sunday so I didn't drink a lot of water, and on Wednesday I wasn't feeling well so I drank a lot of. Even though I know that drinking pure water is important for the human body, I still forget from time to time, and data like this is a good way reminds me.
