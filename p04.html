Project #4 EXQUISITE CORPSE

Discription:

Exquisite corpse is a surrealist technique where players take turns to draw a portion of a figure while not being able to see what has already been drawn by other players. The drawing gets revealed at the end, which tends to create surprising and unexpected forms and narratives. This week, my group members and I exchanged figures with each other, The following three pictures are characters drawn by Ziyan, Amal and myself.

I don't know much about surrealist art, but I like the way it makes me feel. It's like a dream, weird and tender, chaotic and elegant. So in our broken The painting style (very interesting: ziyan painted abstract, amal painted impressionist), if you mix them together, it seems to create a character in the dream.

I tried to convert the svg code of my images to p5 format, but the images were too complicated and did not succeed. So I learned to import images directly. I split the three characters into three parts, drew them using 27 combinations with function(), and played these 27 combinations randomly with random(). The difficulty I encountered was that the positions drawn by everyone were not uniform, and each part of the character generated needed to be adjusted. I could not directly use the random function, but instead the use of function() made function draw() more concise.
