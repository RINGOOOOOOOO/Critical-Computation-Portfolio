Project #2 FORM GENERATOR

Description

Build a simple generator that makes multiple variations of the same form. My first vision was to create a black cat, and its body would dodge the mouse and morph when the mouse was close to it. But I didn't know what kind of tool to use to simulate a cat's bristled hair, so in the end, I changed it to a cat being held in the middle by the mouse and stretched up. But in the process, I realized that the rectangle was difficult to bend, so I drew the cat's body with arc(). But after I tried to stretch the semi-circle drawn by arc() I found it very unnatural, so I changed it to follow the mouse-x-y in small increments. Finally I settled on making a cat that can feed a lying on a table.
